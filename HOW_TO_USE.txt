HOW TO USE: vocal-to-synth-lead
==================================================

PURPOSE
  Dedicated vocal-to-MIDI melody transcriber that turns singing, humming,
  whistling, voice memos, and studio acapellas into clean, expressive monophonic
  MIDI melodies ready to drive synthesizers and virtual instruments.

CAPABILITIES
  - Breath and lip-smack rejection: raised onset threshold (0.60) and 65 Hz - 1100 Hz frequency window
  - Strict monophonic guarantee: eliminates accidental polyphonic double-notes for smooth synth glide
  - Vocal pitch bend smoothing: filters out micro-jitter and throat tremors while keeping slides and vibrato
  - Hard-tune mode (`--no-bends`): strips all pitch bends for rigid, grid-quantized dance/pop synth leads
  - Local File Mode: saves `.mid` directly adjacent to source audio file
  - DAW Integration: stages MIDI clips in Ableton User Library (`Samples/Basic Pitch Leads`) with optional OSC launch

USE CASES
  - Hum or whistle a melody into your phone and convert the voice memo into a synth lead MIDI clip
  - Turn an acapella vocal track into a doubled synth lead line (e.g. Daft Punk / EDM vocal lead layer)
  - Transcribe vocal hooks into MIDI notes for score notation or sheet music lead sheets
  - Drive monophonic analog synthesizers with natural human vocal phrasing and glissandos
  - Quantize rough vocal guide tracks into rigid electronic arp leads

IDEAS
  - Chain with vocal-separation: extract a clean acapella first, then run vocal-to-synth-lead to capture the melody
  - Layer the resulting MIDI track with a Serum or Diva saw lead right underneath the lead vocal in Ableton
  - Use `--no-bends` mode to turn soulful vocal riffs into snappy 8-bit chiptune or arpeggiated synth lines
  - Convert vocal beatbox or scatting melodies into monophonic bass synth tracks
  - Feed the transcribed MIDI into ableton-midi-chord-builder or reharmonization-engine to harmonize the vocal melody

  2. Execute `vocal_lead.py` with vocal-optimized onset and frequency thresholds (65 Hz - 1100 Hz).
  3. Enforce strict monophonic note transitions (no overlapping notes).
  4. Apply moving-average smoothing to pitch bends.
  5. Return clickable markdown links to the generated `.mid` file with note count summary.
